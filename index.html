How to play

📜

Learn in order

Each tower follows a calculus unit in the textbook’s chapter order: a lesson, a multiple-choice check, a written reflection, then a wizard encounter.

🗺️

Open the map

Finish the first tower to unlock the next. Your progress stays saved in this browser.

⚔️

Challenge the wizard

Answer correctly to earn an attack. Pick a calculus spell to lower the boss's health and claim the tower seal.
